# Human Factors in Cybersecurity

Exploring how people think, feel and make decisions when faced with digital threats, persuasion and uncertainty.

Created and maintained by Tegan DeClark, founder of Cybernao, university lecturer, speaker, cyber awareness educator and consultant based in Melbourne, Australia.

My work connects consumer behaviour, social engineering and experiential learning. I am particularly interested in how trust, emotion, social pressure and context influence our decisions and how we can help people navigate those situations with greater confidence.

Through Cybernao, I design and deliver immersive cyber awareness experiences that give people opportunities to practise recognising manipulation, questioning assumptions and responding to challenging situations.

## Areas of focus

- **Social engineering:** persuasion, impersonation, trust and emotional manipulation.
- **Scam resilience:** recognising pressure, checking information and seeking support.
- **Consumer behaviour:** the motivations and circumstances that shape decisions.
- **Cyber awareness education:** practical learning, storytelling and immersive simulations.
- **Human factors:** how individual, social and organisational conditions influence security.

## About this repository

I am developing this collection to share original articles, research commentary and practical learning resources for educators, practitioners, community organisations and people interested in the human side of cybersecurity.
